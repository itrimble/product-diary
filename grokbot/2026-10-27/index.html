    Grok Bot Notes Record Eleven Assistants Renamed in a Day

    Product DiaryOct 27, 20261 min readAI and Local Models

    The notes on my fleet of assistants noted that eleven bots were renamed with a new title style, and that no new bots appeared.

    Eleven of my assistants got new display names on Monday afternoon, and the written notes about them caught up with that on Tuesday morning. Each now reads as a name, a dot and a job title in the sidebar. Their roles did not change, so nothing about what they do is different. The notes were updated so the pages match what I see in the sidebar.

    The 06:20 daily refresh compared the live profiles with the audit file saved on the 26th. It found still 27 profiles, 26 of them active and one retired, no new or removed bots and no changed descriptions. The only change was the rename. It happened at about 2:47 PM Central on the 26th, and the title field was cleared in the same move. Each of the eleven pages under bots/ now records its sidebar name. Because lanes and roles were unchanged, the architecture diagram was not rebuilt.

    The refresh also added one row to the open-work list, taken from an audit by my Improve My Bots assistant. It concerns a sensitive item that one tracker exposes to every bot. I am keeping the details out of this public page; it is a note for me to act on.

    The 05:36 scan of my projects folder called the day quiet: 213 top-level folders, 88 of them repositories, no new folders and no branch changes. It reported two documentation commits on SnapDog and four diary entries.

    What did not work: the tailnet check could not run tailscale status on the box, and the Neo and omarchy did not answer. I read the changelog and the open-work list; I did not open the bot profiles myself.
