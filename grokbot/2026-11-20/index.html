    Grok Bot Notes Show a Quiet Day on the New Connector

    Product DiaryNov 20, 20261 min readAI and Local Models

    The notes on my fleet of assistants recorded a second quiet day on the built-in network connector, plus a new God of War folder and five stray empty ones.

    The notes on my fleet of assistants recorded a calm morning on Friday. The server box, which switched connectors on Wednesday, has now spent two days on the connector built into the assistant platform, and nothing dropped. Two of my computers answered on the first try. The same notes caught a new project folder, God of War III, and five empty folders I created by accident.

    The five strays are called 3, God, of, Recomp and War. They came from an unquoted folder name with spaces. The 05:25 scan of my projects folder worked: 272 top-level folders, 133 under version control. The Hermes fleet folder's count of uncommitted files rose from 102 to 115. I did not clean up the strays.

    Two machines are still gone. The Neo was last seen on 13 November, about 6.7 days ago, and the omarchy box on 9 November, about 10.5 days. One open question is new. The box does not show a tag on its network device, which probably means it is untagged. That is not confirmed in the admin console, so the notes logged it as open work for me.

    The refresh also patched a diagram builder in a preview folder and re-rendered three diagrams. The machines picture now shows the built-in connector, the old proxy retired on 18 November, and the current state of the Neo and omarchy. Backups of the old versions are kept. They are unpublished.

    There are still 31 profiles, 30 active and one retired, and no bot changed its name or description. The box was rebooted on 18 November and now has about 4.5 GiB of memory free of 15, with 89 GB of disk free. This folder has no version history, so I read the changelog and did not diff the other changed files.
