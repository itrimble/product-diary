Prism Shift

Prism Shift is a music-driven puzzle game for the iPhone. You build crystal energy networks, and a line sweeping the board in time with the soundtrack ignites them in chain reactions. Fifteen levels across three worlds each have their own music, scenery and piece mix, ending in a three-phase finale. It is being prepared for a phone-only release.
