Golfprompt is Quiet Links, a 3D golf game that runs in a web browser. It has two courses, a three-press swing, a caddie, a golfer you can dress yourself, and play by mouse, keyboard, game controller or phone touch. An optional ray tracing setting on desktop adds reflections, refraction and contact shading to the pond. It is still in testing, and nobody has played a full round by hand yet.
