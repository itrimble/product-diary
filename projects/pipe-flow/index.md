Pipe Flow, also called Plumber Puzzle, is a water-pipe puzzle where you turn tiles to connect a tap to its drains before the pressure builds and the pipes leak. The iPhone and iPad game is out on the App Store with 140 levels and several modes. A second build made in Godot brings it to Windows, Mac and handheld machines, and it is being matched to the phone game for feel, sound, menus and language support; the game's website carries a playable three-board demo.
