Prism Shift, now being published under the name Night Chorus, is a music-driven puzzle game for the iPhone and Apple TV. You build crystal energy networks, and a line sweeping the board in time with the soundtrack ignites them in chain reactions; matched groups settle into solid colored regions before they clear. Fifteen worlds across 62 stages each have their own music and scenery, and a second version in Godot is catching up to the Apple one. The soundtrack is homebrew and not yet cleared for sale, so a public release is still pending.
