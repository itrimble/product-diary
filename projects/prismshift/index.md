Prism Shift is a music-driven puzzle game for the iPhone and Apple TV. You build crystal energy networks, and a line sweeping the board in time with the soundtrack ignites them in chain reactions; matched groups settle into solid colored regions before they clear. Fifteen worlds across 62 stages each have their own music and scenery, and on the TV the game runs on a wide board with remote or gamepad control. The soundtrack is homebrew and not yet cleared for sale, so a public release is still pending.
