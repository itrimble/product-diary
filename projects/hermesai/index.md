Hermes AI is the working archive of the Hermes assistant fleet that runs on my Mac mini: its skills, its handover notes and the artifacts its scheduled jobs leave behind. The fleet's eleven cron jobs are currently all paused after a November audit found the scheduler's "completed" mark was a weak signal, with one job reporting work that never reached the disk. The repo holds the audit's job-by-job pages, ledgers and raw transcript backup.
