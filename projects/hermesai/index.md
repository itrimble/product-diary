Hermes AI is the working archive of the Hermes assistant fleet that runs on my Mac mini: its skills, its handover notes and the artifacts its scheduled jobs leave behind. The fleet's eleven cron jobs are currently all paused after a November audit found the scheduler's "completed" mark was a weak signal. The repo now also holds a benchmark kit for comparing small local coding models, which has finished its warm-up checks but has not yet scored a full run.
