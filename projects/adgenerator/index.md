AdGenerator turns recordings of my apps into short promotional videos. It captures a demo, crops it to the good parts, then lays titles, captions, a generated voiceover and music over it, and exports widescreen and phone-shaped videos plus a GIF. Its first reel is a sixteen-second SnapDog advert that opens on an email left visible in a capture and closes on blurring it. The folder still has no version control.
