Remnant Security is the public website for a cybersecurity career-training business: courses, career paths, and a ninety-day plan builder that offers a free 2026 roadmap PDF for joining the mailing list. Sign-ups go through the academy service so account credentials stay off the public pages. The site builds as a static bundle, deploys from main, and keeps its rules in its README, SECURITY.md and Handover.md.
