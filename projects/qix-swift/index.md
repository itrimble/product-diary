QixForge is an iPhone, iPad and Apple TV take on the classic Qix territory game, where you claim ground by drawing lines while avoiding a roaming monster. It has ten sectors, each ending in a boss fight, plus power-ups, a neon look, high-score boards, story scenes and music that reacts to how you play. All 100 levels are balanced and validated, there are nine color-vision modes, and it is in App Store submission prep under the name Qix Sectors.
