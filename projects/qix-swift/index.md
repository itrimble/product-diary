QixForge is an iPhone, iPad and Apple TV take on the classic territory game, where you claim ground by drawing lines while avoiding a roaming monster. Its public name is now Lightfence. It has ten sectors, each ending in a boss fight, power-ups, a hands-on tutorial, nine color-vision modes and music that reacts to how you play. It is translated into 13 languages, and its App Store listing is being prepared.
