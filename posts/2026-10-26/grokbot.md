The written notes about my fleet of assistants caught up on Monday morning after going stale for five days, and in the catch-up two new helpers turned up. One is meant to clear election mail out of my inbox. The other is meant to make real phone calls for me, such as cancellations, refunds and reservations. The notes say plainly that neither has been proven to work yet.

The daily refresh did not land from the 21st to the 25th, because Sunday's run failed. The 06:20 run found the gap by comparing the live profiles with an audit file saved on the 20th. Political Spam Blocker was written on the 20th at about 8:12 PM, Phone Call Bot on the 21st at about 1:17 AM. Both are short template profiles with no chat history. The README now counts 27 profiles, 26 of them active, and the architecture diagram says 26.

The cautious part matters more. A STOP reply to a campaign text goes out in my name, so the notes say it waits for my approval. For the phone bot, I do not know whether its profile asks before each call or has switched on the mode that calls alone, so the notes default to asking first. No phone numbers go in the docs.

The refresh also flagged 26 open work items that had not moved in more than a week. It rebuilt a fleet-map preview for 26 bots but did not publish it. I read the changelog and bot pages; I did not open the profiles myself, and the claim that both bots have no history is the refresh's, not mine.
