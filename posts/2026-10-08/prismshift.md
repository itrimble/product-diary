---
project: Prism Shift
title: Prism Shift's Fifteen Levels Get Their Own Music
summary: All fifteen levels now have their own music, backdrops and layouts, and the music slides from one level into the next without stopping play.
---
Clear a level in Prism Shift and the game no longer makes you wait. The next level's music fades in over the old track while you keep playing. Until this change the game felt like three places. Now every one of its fifteen levels has its own tune, crystal layout and scenery, with fresh paintings for worlds two and three and a different crystal cut in each world.

The cave was the weak spot. Ten levels already had recorded scores, while the other five, the cave levels, made do with thin synthesized loops that faded out and then cut back in. Each now has a separate composition, with its own melody, bass and rhythm, in stereo. They use the same overlapping hand-off as the recorded scores, and play resumes the instant the incoming track starts.

Every level also got a design record covering its piece mix, a shape family and a landmark drawn behind the board. First Light has blossoms, Neon District has broken signals and Crystal Caverns has a heartstone altar. A new piece mix means old balance numbers no longer apply, so I re-measured all fifteen targets from the original five seeds. The Heartstone ceiling failed the +20% check, so the baseline for the whole game was rebuilt. I loosened no gate.

For listening there is a medley of all fifteen levels with 0.8-second crossfades, plus fatigue loops of 5, 15 and 30 minutes. All fifty stems pass the loop and headroom checks. The ten recorded scores hash differently, which proves they are different files and nothing about quality. Nobody has heard this on a real phone, and the licensing for the recorded music is unresolved. Nothing here is under version control, so for now the work exists only on disk.
