project: Prism Shift
title: Prism Shift Gets New Music for All Fifteen Levels
summary: All fifteen levels now play brand-new music made on the home computer, and the game was installed on a real phone.
---
Prism Shift now has a new soundtrack. Each of the fifteen levels got its own piece of music, made on the Mac mini by a music-writing program, and all fifteen are switched on in the game for Ian's personal use. Every level also now has its own look: a backdrop, a moving landmark, a lighting tint and a distinct way the crystals are cut. The updated game was installed on the phone. How the music actually sounds in play has not been judged by a person yet.

Two things limit what this build is. The music model is licensed for non-commercial use only, and Ian confirmed on the 11th that this is a homebrew project. The notes say plainly that these tracks are not cleared for selling the game, and the model's credit has to stay. Also, the model produces a finished mix, not the five separate layers the game's engine normally blends. So each track plays as one loop in a folder called `SkinTracks`, and the old layered recordings stay in the project as the fallback.

The notes are in `docs/YUE2_HOME_BREW_SOUNDTRACK.md` and `docs/YUE2_SKIN_AUDITIONS.md`. All fifteen drafts, normalized masters and prepared loops exist. The loops passed an audit of the fifteen files for format and clipping. Beat and key review and a five-minute listen are still pending. The notes warn that a requested tempo is not evidence of the real tempo, so none was trusted.

Two game modes were also described. Challenge uses earned-light goals with no clock. Single Skin loops one level you have reached at its own tempo with no goals and no speed-up, though running out of room still ends a run. The matching rule and the six-beat sweep were left alone on purpose: changing the sweep to eight beats would need the balance numbers measured again, and a question about changing matches to 2×2 squares is still open.
