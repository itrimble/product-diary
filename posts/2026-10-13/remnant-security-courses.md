The teaching materials got a home of their own today. The curriculum for the training business, every lesson, lab, assessment and recording plan, had been sitting in a folder with no version history at all; it is now the canonical source of record for what the academy teaches. Nothing a learner sees changed. The safety net did.

Commit `049475e` is the initial history. The repo carries the curriculum for both products, the Career Jumpstart course and the flagship bootcamp, plus the generated technical lab workbook, worked examples of the portfolio artifacts learners are asked to build, and the production skill that assembles new course material. The same ignore rules as the academy's keep secrets out: environment files, keys and certificates are excluded from day one. The assessments stay private material, so this entry names their existence and quotes none of them.

The day's two first commits, this and the academy app's, belong with the afternoon's cleanup on the public site: the email key removed from its public code and the PDF sources pulled off the web root. The pattern was consolidation, moving scattered work into homes where publishing it by accident stops being possible.
