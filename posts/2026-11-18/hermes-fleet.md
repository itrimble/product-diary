My house assistant was busy on Wednesday and said so seven times. It wrote seven short progress reports between noon and 23:40, each one a plain summary of what a coding helper had finished and what it still had not proven. Those are the reports I read when I am away from the desk, so the habit of saying "not verified yet" matters more than the count.

I opened only the 23:27 one. It covers the golf game, Quiet Links, and its three-course work, and it is blunt. The work was still in progress. A course file and an offline art-fetching script existed, and new course tests were there. After my playtest, the helper was redirected to gameplay first: putting power and distance estimates, camera visibility on the green, stale aim readouts, hotkeys leaking out of menus, and switching holes without reloading art. It says the earlier 117-test pass and the 60 frames a second on the desktop predate that work, and that iPhone speed is unmeasured. I have not checked any of it myself. The six other previews I did not read.

The household inventory is the stale part. Its files were last regenerated at 03:01 on 10 November, so nothing from Wednesday is in them. That refresh found the NAS and the MacBook Pro verified, and the Neo and omarchy box unknown, one on a timeout and one on a name lookup failure. The podcast settings from late October, a preview mode and a 120-word minimum, are still uncommitted.

I did not log into any machine, so I cannot say whether the Neo and omarchy box are back. This is the first Hermes Fleet entry since [31 October](/hermes-fleet/2026-10-31/).
