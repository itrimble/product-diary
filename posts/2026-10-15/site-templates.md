title: "Site Templates: A Shared Starter Kit for Product Sites"
summary: A shared starter kit for the company's product web sites was built, with one layout for apps and one for games.
---
Product sites no longer have to start from a blank page. Built individually, they had drifted apart, in appearance and in how easily search engines found them. The new shared kit gives each product a starting point: one layout for apps, one for games. Both handle a looping clip, screenshots, a row of stats and a table. StatBar's site was the proving ground, and the kit was cut from it.

Here is the order it came together in. A copy of the StatBar site became the template, then got a new name. The next commit did the heavy lifting: the two presets, the video, gallery, stats and table blocks, a step that renders pages ahead of time, overrides for each site, and scripts to encode clips and assemble a highlight reel. The written rules came after: design guidance, a playbook for screenshots and reels, and a catalogue recording which art and footage is safe to publish. Nine products got previews built on it: StatBar, SnapDog, Lectern, CyberSim, Plumber Puzzle, Window Switcher, TilePilot, Lightfence and Prism Shift.

The final commit tracks the template's placeholder media, which stops a fresh copy from failing on missing files. Nothing from the kit went public that day. Previews stay local, since pushing a site repository deploys it. I suspect the publishing catalogue will prove the most useful part of the kit.
