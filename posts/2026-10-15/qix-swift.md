title: Lightfence Menus Now Read From the Couch on Apple TV
summary: Lightfence's menus now read from the couch on Apple TV, and the tutorial teaches the game's pickups and the glowing orb.
---
On a television, Lightfence (the game formerly called QixForge) asked too much of your eyes, and 15 October fixed that. Menu words that were tiny from across a room now reach the size Apple recommends. Arabic and other long translations no longer crash into buttons and switches. The tutorial has grown too. It introduces the glowing orb, the Nexus and the power-ups one by one, so a late level stops piling captions on a new player.

Start with the size problem, which I find the most telling. Apple sets a floor of 23 points for text on tvOS, and the menu sub-screens were drawing at 12 to 20. Their layouts could scale up by 1.6 at most. A television now lays them out like a phone turned sideways, at roughly 2.2 on a 1920 by 1080 screen, which puts options, scores and settings labels at 22 to 25 points. Phones and iPads stay as they were. TV Options also now says RUMBLE, the same word as Settings.

The caption trouble appeared on a fresh save. Level 27 threw the Snarl, the Tracers, mini-Snarls, the orb and the Nexus at the player simultaneously. Now the tutorial coach waits at least three seconds before naming anything new. The new orb, Nexus and power-up lessons arrived with 73 lines of tests.

Text bugs got the rest of the attention. Arabic is never set in a monospaced face now, on game screens or SwiftUI ones. Settings switches used to cram "ON" and "OFF" at either end into about 16 points, so they show only the current word, in the room the knob leaves. The Portuguese "OFF" read as "none"; it is "DESLIGADO" now. And picking a stage on the sector map dives you into it. I would call the TV sizing the change that matters most.
