title: SnapDog Gets New Ad Research After Its Own Notes Were Wrong
summary: The app did not change, but I wrote new ad copy for SnapDog after finding my own notes still called the shipped app unreleased.
---
SnapDog itself did not change on Wednesday. What did change is what I say about it. I checked the live Mac App Store page while writing a new batch of advertising copy, and found my own project notes were wrong: they still described the app as coming soon and waiting for review, when it has been on sale since 25 September.

The new file is `marketing/ads/native_sales_ads_2026-10-28.md`, about 1,750 words. It opens with a corrections table. The store shows version 1.2.1 at $14.99, a "Data Not Collected" privacy label, 13 languages and no ratings yet. The README's launch-week $9.99 price is not on the page, so no ad may use it. The README also promises 19 upload destinations, but the shipped listing names a shorter set, so the copy sticks to what the store says.

The research found a gap. A CleanShot X alternatives roundup, updated on 24 October, covers nine competitors and does not mention SnapDog. It says people leave that app over the cloud, the price, or wanting a better recorder, so the positioning is a one-time, local-first tool with no account. There are five audiences and several ad variants, each tagged with a channel and buyer stage.

Two limits. The market claims come from search snippets and forum threads, and the file says so; I did not open every link, and it makes no traffic or competitor-share claims. Also, my local checkout sits 362 commits ahead of the public repository, so anything that exists only locally is marked as not shipping.

I did not try to photograph the app tonight; the build step failed on a simulator that was not available.
