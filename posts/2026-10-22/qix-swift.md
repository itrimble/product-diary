title: Lightfence Gets a Tabletop Layout for Folding Phones
summary: Lightfence gained a tabletop layout for a folding phone, and build 1004 went to App Store review.
---
Prop a folding phone on a table, half open like a little laptop, and Lightfence now uses both halves sensibly. The upright half is the screen you look at. Everything you touch sits on the flat half, which holds the score columns and a steering pad. A second finger on that pad fires the overdrive boost. Pause and result cards drop down there as well, so the crease never cuts through them. Hold the same phone open like a book and the game is unchanged. App Store review now has build 1004.

The crease is the interesting part. The game asks the system where the fold is, which needs iOS 27.1, and asks again whenever the hinge moves. A new rule called a posture treats a crease running across a sideways view as a tabletop. The playing field is still shaped as though the phone were fully open, so a stage is identical whether it is propped or held, and setting the phone down in the middle of one keeps your run. The heads-up display moved to the flat half too, and the power-up slots squeeze together to suit a short column. The string catalog grew by 156 lines.

The simulator has no hinge, so a launch option invents one for testing. A UI test built on it covers the iPad and the Duo's open size, asking whether the board stays clear of the crease and whether tapping the pad steers. I have not run it.

Seven Duo-simulator captures exist at 1398 by 2034, from the title screen through Aqua Reef, Neon City, Snarl Prime, the sector map, Light Core and Films. They are filed away, not live. Screenshots for version 1.2 are locked during its review, so these wait for the next version.
