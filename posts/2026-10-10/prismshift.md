project: Prism Shift
title: Prism Shift Starts Relaxed and Adds Color-Vision Shapes
summary: Relaxed became the starting pace for new players, and color-vision options now mark crystals with shapes as well as colors.
---
New Prism Shift players now start on the gentler Relaxed setting, where pieces fall and the rising tide climbs fifty percent more slowly. Music and scoring are untouched, and anyone who already chose a difficulty keeps it. The second change helps players with color-vision differences: eight modes recolor the crystals, and each crystal carries a symbol of its own, so color is never the only clue. I am aiming at Sunday the 11th for release, but nothing has been installed or played on the phone yet. How any of this feels is unknown to me.

My case for the new default is a bot experiment, written up in a decision note. Fifteen levels, two seeds, with Relaxed and without. Every one of the 30 runs survived on both settings, and median progress toward the energy target came to 0.426 against 0.405. That says nothing certain about humans, and the note admits it. The bug it exposed was worth more. Every run finished by surviving, yet scenery and music followed only energy, so they stopped short of the final phase. Progress now counts whichever of energy or survival is higher.

The symbols are a circle, triangle, square, plus and bars, one for each of the five colors. People with red or green deficiencies get a blue and amber palette. White symbols sit on black badges, which keeps them legible against light crystals as well as dark ones.

On the 10th the full test gate passed 629 tests across 93 suites. Thirty-six of 40 interface flows passed first time, the other four were fixed, and twelve rechecks passed. I still would not call it done. The final audit lists completion as unproven: two phone installs of that build were interrupted, a newer build did install, and proof of the music licensing is still missing.
