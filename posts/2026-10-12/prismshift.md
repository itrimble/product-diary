project: Prism Shift
title: Prism Shift Chain Reactions Get a Camera-Zoom Payoff
summary: Chain reactions now trigger a big camera-zoom flourish, blocks pulse softly to the music, and each level's scenery lasts across several stages.
---
The best moves in Prism Shift now get a proper reward. A large chain of crystals sends the camera sweeping in toward the board, with sparkles and rising notes that follow the song, and the blocks swell and fade a little in step with the beat. The fifteen worlds also last longer. Each one spans several stages, so its scenery and music keep going rather than starting over. None of this has reached my phone, because I asked for the phone to be left alone until I am back.

So the evidence is all from a computer. The 62 stages divide as four each for thirteen worlds and five each for the final two, and simulated runs through all of them finished in 30 of 30 attempts. The pulse never grows past 1.5% in size or 3% in opacity, rides the beat clock the game already had, and stops entirely under Reduce Motion. The new "fever" zoom has ten focused checks, and a 20 second recording of the master audio output stayed clean, with no clipping.

Several rule fixes went in with it. A test override had quietly limited play to squares, and the six piece shapes work again. Any connected cluster of three or more same-kind blocks now stays whole. Special blocks fire only as part of a charged match, after the chain finishes. Matched blocks regained their white outlines, and a stage ending no longer drops the piece in mid-fall.

My label for all this is "built and checked on a computer". A signed release exists but is not installed. The videos and audio captures are separate files with no sync. Whether the pulse is gentle enough and the zoom comfortable can only be judged by someone holding the phone, and I would not call it finished before that.
