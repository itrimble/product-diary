---
project: Civic Fortune 2026
title: "Civic Fortune 2026: Three Ways to Win Now Work"
summary: Three of the game's five ways to win could never be won; they now can, and a run saves itself so you can quit and come back.
---
Nobody had told me that three of Civic Fortune 2026's five victories were out of reach, because the game never said so. A second problem sat beside it: close the app and the run was gone, which stings when a full game lasts 52 weeks. Both are fixed. Leave in the middle of a run and the menu now offers to continue from the week you left. Every victory can be earned, and people who rely on the phone's screen reader can finally press the shop's buy button.

The dead victories came from the way Swift hashes strings. The result differs on every launch, so each run drew a different game from the same content. The balance probe never asserted anything about the win rates it printed, so a broken game could not fail it. A fixed hash (FNV-1a) now stands in at both places that used the old one, and two probe runs produce byte-identical output. A further test greps the source and fails if the old call turns up outside a comment.

Real figures only appeared once the routing stopped moving. Mutual Resilience won every run, on week 4. Financial Runway, Influence and Stabilized won none. The same asset could be bought without limit, since nothing marked it unique. To win on Runway you needed the lease asset, and it drains 60 cash a week while the victory gate sits at 2,400. The cheap baseline wins fired on week 8 and pre-empted the costly ones. Designed wins therefore cannot happen before week 16, and the baseline cannot before week 28.

The autosave is a store called RunStore, which writes one atomic slot in Application Support. A save written by a newer format is refused, and a corrupt file puts a message on the menu. A finished run wipes that slot, and the end screen gained a Play Again button. To test it I played 7 weeks and then killed the app. The menu came back offering "Continue Run, Week 8". Taps on routes, event answers, Rest and Buy now give haptic feedback. VoiceOver could not reach Buy because the shop row merged all of its contents; only the description merges now, and the button reads "Buy Side Hustle for 240 dollars". All 47 tests pass.
