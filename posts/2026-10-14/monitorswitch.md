---
project: Monitor Switch
summary: A monitor that refused to follow the desk switch to the personal laptop was traced to a cable, and unplugging it fixed it.
---
One monitor on the desk would not follow the switch box when it was flipped to the personal laptop. Ian had to pick the right input in the monitor's own menu by hand every time. The cause turned out to be one cable. The work laptop's docking station was also plugged into that monitor, and the monitor stayed loyal to it even when nothing useful was coming through. With that cable unplugged, the monitor follows the switch on its own. No software was needed.

The monitor is a budget onn 24-inch. Its Auto Source setting only falls back to the next input when the current one goes dead. The Targus DisplayLink dock keeps its HDMI link alive with a blank picture, so the monitor never sees a loss. The evidence is a repeat test: with the HDMI cable out, one press of the switch moved the monitor over, and with it back in on a different port the problem returned.

Several software routes were tried and ruled out. Asking the monitor to change input over its control channel failed on all three monitors, with the same general I/O error, so the switch box and dock probably block that channel. Stopping the DisplayLink software on the work laptop, both by killing the agent and by quitting its app, blanked the picture but did not release the monitor. One earlier test was run on the wrong machine and was retracted in the notes. A tool that was tried also crashes when the small prompter display is attached.

Still untested: the return trip back to the work laptop and then back again, and whether putting the work laptop to sleep releases the monitor. If the work laptop needs a picture while the switch sits on the personal one, the notes say to plug that cable into a different monitor.
