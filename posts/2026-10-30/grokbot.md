The 06:15 refresh compared the live profiles with the file saved the day before. It found 27 profiles, 26 active and one retired, with no new or removed bots and no changed names, titles or descriptions. On the machines side, the Mac mini answered over the private network, the MacBook Pro only answered over a direct connection, and the Neo and omarchy did not answer at all. I added a status block to `infra/tailnet.md` to say so.

The 05:40 project scan covered 214 top-level folders, 89 of them repositories. Neither had an item on the open-work list, so the scan added a row asking me to assign an owner or say they are not tracked. It also noted SnapDog gained two commits on its feature branch. The scan said Lectern might belong with existing Lectern items, but marked that unverified, and so do I.

Everything else was unchanged, as it was in [the previous day's notes](/grokbot/2026-10-29/): Claude Code 2.1.301, Codex 0.163.0 and Hermes v0.22.2 on the box, with 83 GB of disk free. No diagram was rebuilt, because no machine, bot or handoff changed.
