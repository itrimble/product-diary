title: SnapDog Rewrites Its Website Films Around Remote Help
summary: The Mac app's help menu now opens the SnapDog website, and the site's demo films were re-planned twice to tell one story about helping someone remotely.
---
The help menu in the Mac version of SnapDog now opens the product's own website, and the About panel lists the tools and people behind the app. The larger change of the day is on paper: the plan for the website's demonstration films was torn up twice in one evening until it settled on one story, a person rescuing somebody else's computer from far away.

The storyboard lives in `website/VIDEO_STORYBOARD.md`. It was started at 20:10 with 77 lines, torn down nine minutes later and re-centred on helping someone remotely, then rebuilt by 20:36 into one full product story. Three passes in twenty-six minutes is not indecision; it is a plan converging from a tour of features to one customer, one problem, one rescue.

The app-side change came earlier, in the afternoon: Help and About in the menu bar now lead to the site, with a new credits view behind the About item. This follows [the English demo film recorded on 14 October](/snapdog/2026-10-14/), and the films now being storyboarded are the website's own. The reel that faces prospects, I note with a sigh, still speaks German.
