Epochs of Power, my long-running research project toward a touch-first historical strategy game built on Call to Power II, spent the day in the archive rather than the workshop. The study of the original game's inner workings grew by another section, the register of its artwork gained entries, and the scripts that run the 2000 game inside a windowless virtual display were hardened, so it can be probed automatically instead of by hand.

The source-port study, the project's central document, took 44 new lines, and the asset register took 35 more. The two scripts that changed are the unglamorous kind that make research repeatable: one prepares the old game's runtime, the other drives it under a virtual display and received a round of fixes. A small image-model script for iterating on generated artwork moved forward too. Modest numbers, but this project advances by documentation, and the documentation advanced.

For anyone arriving here for the first time: the plan is a personal, touch-first strategy game in the spirit of the Call to Power line, built on studying the original's released source rather than guessing at it. Nothing is playable yet, and today moved the study, not the game. The work is sitting uncommitted in the folder, which is how research days often end.
