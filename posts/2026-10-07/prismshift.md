project: Prism Shift
title: Prism Shift Passes Every Walkthrough on a Small Phone
summary: Every scripted walkthrough of the whole game now passes on a small phone screen, three days before its planned release.
---
Prism Shift is a new phone puzzle game, and this is its first appearance in the diary. You place crystals on a board to build energy networks. A line then sweeps across in time with the music and ignites them, setting off chain reactions. Release is planned for Sunday, and on 7 October it was three days away. By the end of that day, scripted checks had played the entire game on a small phone screen, with all 31 guided walkthroughs passing. Rewards now survive closing the app, and the ending and credits have screens of their own.

At 18:24 the last run finished: 579 tests, 74 suites, every one green. The walkthroughs used a compact phone simulator and went through the menu, settings, all three worlds, the three-phase finale, the darkness counter-play, Abyss mode, graphics modes, reduced motion, the three control schemes, tutorials, credits and the ending. The first complete pass got 28 of the 31 through. The credits had a Close button that scrolled away with the page, and two settings screens had controls out of thumb reach. Those were the three failures. Once fixed, all seven reruns of the affected walkthroughs passed. Numbered screenshots and a video of the finale record the whole trail.

The testing changed the game as well. Achievements now number eight, each with a generated illustration. They are awarded during play, at level hand-offs and when a run ends, and they persist across relaunches. You can replay the opening and the three-part finale. A banner between levels used to cover the board on small phones and no longer does. Falling crystals no longer get rebuilt sixty times a second, so their animation carries over between moves. Music used to cut dead after a clear; it now eases out over 450 milliseconds. The build is now declared as a release for phones only.

Open items before Sunday: I have yet to learn how the controls feel on a physical phone. Distribution signing and the beta upload are not done, and the licensing for the recorded music stems still needs sorting. There is also no version control on the project at all, and I would sooner fix that before release day than after.
