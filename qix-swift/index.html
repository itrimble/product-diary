QixForge

QixForge is an iPhone and iPad take on the classic Qix territory game, where you claim ground by drawing lines while avoiding a roaming monster. It has ten sectors with bosses, power-ups, a neon look, high-score boards, per-sector music and short story scenes. All 100 levels are balanced and validated end to end, and it is in App Store submission prep under the name Qix Sectors.
