    QixForge

    QixForge is an iPhone, iPad and Apple TV take on the classic territory game, where you claim ground by drawing lines while avoiding a roaming monster. Its public name is now Lightfence. It has ten sectors, each ending in a boss fight, power-ups, a hands-on tutorial, nine color-vision modes and music that reacts to how you play. It is translated into 13 languages; version 1.2 is out on the App Store, version 1.3 with fully repainted high-resolution art is in review, and an unsigned Mac build candidate for Steam exists but has not been uploaded anywhere.
