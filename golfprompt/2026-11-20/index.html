    Quiet Links Golf Gets a Three-Press Swing and a Caddie

    Product DiaryNov 20, 20262 min readApps

    My browser golf game now has a swing you control with three presses, a second course, a caddie and a golfer you can dress yourself.

    My browser golf game, Quiet Links, now plays like golf, following the golfer and phone controls. You press once to start the backswing, again to set the power, and a third time as the marker comes back to the line. Press early and the ball pulls, late and it pushes. There is also a second course, a caddie who picks the club and aims, and a golfer you can dress yourself. Nobody has played it by hand yet, me included.

    The new welcome card on hole 1, captured in Chromium on 20 November 2026, with the three-step swing guide and a clean score bar. The old swing clip lifted a leg and bent the arms the wrong way, so the golfer now holds a posed stance and the club moves with the hands. The ball leaves 325 to 338 milliseconds after the third press. A full driver carries 227.8 metres, which is 249 yards. Courses are Quiet Links (par 4, 3, 5) and Dune Ridge (par 4, 3, 4), with a scorecard and personal bests that are remembered.

    Dune Ridge hole 2 in Chromium on 20 November 2026; the caddie suggests a 9 iron and an 80 percent swing for 136 yards. The caddie holed 144 of 144 putts across 24 spots on each of six greens, and had a clear shot at the pin from 93 of 97 places around the greens. The art download grew from 9.6 MB to 13 MB, and a test caught that the new turf maps took texture memory from 89 MB to 153 MB, because the old test never counted ground textures.

    Today's checks: 204 unit tests and 39 browser tests passed on the MacBook Pro (one run showed 38, until I fixed a 0.99 metre timing race). On a throttled phone profile it ran at 40.2 frames a second. That was in a desktop browser at phone size, not a real phone. A progress report my house assistant wrote just after midnight called the backswing still stiff, and I have not reconciled that with these screenshots.
