    SnapDog

    SnapDog is a Mac app for taking screenshots and recording the screen, with an editor for annotating captures and a history you can filter. It is still being polished: recent work went into recording reliability on the Mac, a Linux port catching up with the Mac app, and the website, which now has a first silent demo film of about two minutes.
