SnapDog

SnapDog is a Mac app for taking screenshots and recording the screen, with an editor for annotating captures and a history you can filter. It is still being polished. Recent work has gone into small details that make editing and recording feel right, such as arrows that point where you drag and click rings that appear under the cursor, and into the website that sells it.
