SnapDog — 2026-10-02

Nothing new reached SnapDog users on this day. The fix that keeps your first click from being wasted when a screen recording starts, written up on September 30, is still sitting unfinished on the Mac mini. The site that sells the app gained new pictures of its order page, but that is the only visible change.

The survey flagged SnapDog because of uncommitted work, but the file dates tell a quieter story. The three focus files, RecordingFocus.swift, its edit to RecordingManager.swift, and RecordingFocusTests.swift, were last saved at about 22:35 the night before. The test file holds three tests for the "which app gets control back" decision. I did not run them, and none of it is committed.

The new website images are order-page.png and order-annotated.png in website/media, both saved around 11:30 the previous morning, next to older shots of the editor and the filtered history view. I could not take a fresh screenshot of the app, because the capture script only photographs today's build and would not show the app as it was that day.
